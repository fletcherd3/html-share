high-bar practice targets

a new shortlist after Fletcher’s clarified exclusions: no HFT/trading, banks, finance/fintech, C++-required roles or C++-required coding rounds; target compensation at least A$130k. This replaces—not repeats—the earlier broad practice-target research.

what is known vs not known: both official listings are currently live and describe non-C++ engineering work. Interview-format reports and compensation figures below are third-party/candidate-reported, not promises. Neither employer’s live listing publishes a Sydney salary band or a guaranteed interview language.

the two decision-ready options

ranked 1 · early/mid-career level
ad-tech

The Trade Desk — Software Engineer II

known from the role: 2+ years’ experience, distributed systems, and proficiency in one or more of C#, Java, C, C++, Python, SQL or Scala. C++ is listed as one option, not a requirement. The work includes algorithmic, optimisation and scale challenges.

likely interview signal: a dated Sydney candidate report (Oct 2025) describes a recruiter screen and four onsite rounds; other reports on the same source describe two roughly LeetCode-medium algorithm questions and a 90-minute assessment. These are anecdotal and may not match this team.

pay is unresolved: Levels.fyi’s Greater Sydney aggregate lists SE II at A$160k base, but a level-specific page shows a lower reported average. Do not assume it clears the A$130k-base floor—confirm early.

why it suits the strategy: strong engineering bar with lower emotional stakes if ad-tech is not a preferred destination.

ranked 2 · senior title
java backend

Canva — Senior Software Engineer, Product & Features (Java)

known from the role: Sydney/remote-ANZ Java backend position covering scalable, reliable services, service decomposition, performance and engineering quality. The posting also says some interviews can include real-time AI-tool challenges. No C++ requirement is stated.

likely interview signal: recent Sydney candidate reports on Glassdoor mention Java screening, topological-sort implementation, binary-tree and binary-search questions, HashMap operations, plus system design. These reports are anecdotal, not Canva policy.

pay confidence is stronger: third-party Greater Sydney compensation data lists Canva B4 base at A$142k; the role’s exact level/band is unpublished, so recruiter confirmation is still required.

why it suits the strategy: probably the cleanest Java/DS&A practice signal, but the senior title and Canva’s desirability could make it less emotionally low-stakes.

smallest useful decision

Pick one target to investigate first. The evidence supports The Trade Desk as the more low-stakes practice bet, if its base-salary and language policy check out; Canva has the clearer Java/algorithmic signal but may be a higher-stakes choice. This page does not recommend applying automatically.

questions that resolve the real uncertainties

  • “What is the Sydney base-salary band for this level?”
  • “Can every coding interview be completed in Java or Python, with no C++ expectation?”
  • “Which rounds are live coding, data structures/algorithms, system design and team match?”
  • “Is this a specific team opening or an evergreen/team-match process?”

sources & limits

  • The Trade Desk official listing — current requirements and technologies.
  • Canva official listing — current requirements and AI-tool interview note.
  • The Trade Desk candidate reports and Canva candidate reports — non-official, dated candidate experience only.
  • Trade Desk pay data and Canva pay data — crowdsourced compensation, not an offer or salary band.
