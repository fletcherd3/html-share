M49 is independently real

Mazzucchelli says M49 reaches 68% natural-origin content and exceeded 90% biodegradation after 115 days in specified tests.

category 3 means something

the ACCC defines it as high sun-glare reduction with good UV protection.

look for the label

Australian stock should identify the supplier, lens category, usage information and applicable warnings.

important: the Vix material and manufacturing claims remain Kupuna’s own. Mazzucchelli independently validates what M49 is, not that a particular Vix batch contains it; only supplier or batch documentation could prove that.[1][6][15][16]

cheap-pair inspection

deal, not dud.

match the identity: Vix / Brindle / Gray / 50–22–145.

ask whether it is new retail stock, display stock, a cosmetic second or old inventory.

get fresh photos of both temples, hinges, lenses, case and packaging.

check for lens chips, coating marks, scratches, hinge play and warped arms.

request a receipt naming the seller, model and disclosed condition.

for private resale, use buyer protection — never blind bank transfer.
